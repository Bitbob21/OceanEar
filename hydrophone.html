Aquarian Audio H2d

The kit ships an Aquarian Audio H2d, made by Aquarian Audio & Scientific, Anacortes, Washington. 3.5 mm TRS output on plug-in power. Signal conditioning is Aquarian’s CS6 board, rated by the manufacturer at about 10 dB better sensitivity and signal-to-noise than the CS4 board in the earlier H2a.

Manufacturer figures for typical response. Gain and high-frequency behaviour on plug-in power follow the input’s bias current and impedance, and the cable length; Aquarian publishes no response plots for that reason.

Cable lengths

  • 6 m: wharf, jetty, dinghy, and below the surface layer.
  • 9 m: from a boat: clear of hull noise and its reflections, and long enough to reach the water from a flybridge.

In the kit

Hydrophone, class-compliant USB-C interface, redeem code for the full app. The interface supplies the plug-in power and bypasses the phone’s combined headset jack, which is not wired for a microphone input.

Recording side: 48 kHz, WAV or AAC, GPS and timestamp per file. Full kit specifications.

Warranty
