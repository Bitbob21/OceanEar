Quick tips

OceanEar works right away with the iPhone’s built-in microphone. For underwater or stereo recordings, connect a hydrophone or stereo mic via 3.5mm or USB-C. Spatial audio with head tracking works best with AirPods Pro, AirPods Max or other motion-enabled headphones.

Your recordings are stored on your device and, if iCloud backup is enabled, mirrored to iCloud Drive so they survive app deletion.
