Workshop

We bring the tank to your class.

The masking experiment from the online unit, run live in your classroom with a real water tank and a hydrophone. The class hears a whale call in clear water, then hears the same call disappear under a passing engine, on the room's own speakers, from water they are standing next to. We come to you anywhere in the Wellington region and bring everything with us. About an hour, built for a full class, nothing for you to set up.

The Edu Kit.

A box that leaves the sound in the room after we have gone. An exciter that turns the tank wall itself into a loudspeaker, a set of animal figures the class can place and listen to one at a time, and a hydrophone option for schools that want to record their own stretch of coast. Built so a teacher can run the whole thing without us in the room.
